Q1

✅ It guides test construction and item allocation.
Q2
Which is NOT one of the four TOS components?
✅ The four are topics, hours spent per topic, total hours, and total number of items.
Q3
Items per topic equals:
✅ More time spent teaching means more items.
Q4
A topic taught for 5 of 20 total hours, with a 40-item test, gets how many items?
✅ (5 ÷ 20) × 40 = 10.
Q5
A topic taught for 4 of 8 total hours, with a 50-item test, gets how many items?
✅ (4 ÷ 8) × 50 = 25.
Q6

✅ A One-Way TOS only has topic, hours, percentage, number of items, and item placement.
